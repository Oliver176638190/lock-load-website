About Us

Company Overview

Shanghai Lock and Load Fluid Transfer Technology Co., Ltd. and Lock and Load Limited are technology-driven enterprises specializing in fluid transfer technology and industrial automation control.

The company integrates technical services, product distribution, equipment manufacturing, and import & export trade, dedicated to delivering comprehensive professional solutions for industrial fluid control systems.

Since its inception, the company has consistently placed technological innovation at its core. Backed by a skilled technical team and extensive industry expertise, we serve clients across chemical, energy, manufacturing, and environmental protection sectors with premium products and technical services.
